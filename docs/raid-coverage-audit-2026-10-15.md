# GO Hub S/A raid coverage audit

Checked October 15, 2026 against app snapshot 2026-10-10-910cf0fdc87a. Includes SS (above S), S and A tiers across all 17 non-Normal attack types. Normal is intentionally excluded. Downloaded the complete embedded lists, not just the first page or top ten. Each list reached tier F, so all qualifying tiers were covered. Default GO Hub settings include mixed movesets, off-type attackers, and Super Max Mega Level; a listing does not independently establish current release or a copy's required unlocks.

## Results

- 397 qualifying type-role entries checked.
- 104 existing catalog forms have no qualifying direct raid recommendation, covering 124 roles.
- 73 additional roles are missing on forms that already have another raid recommendation.
- 4 source forms are entirely absent from the catalog.
- 1 included role has a different recommended moveset.
- 0 source-fetch or parse failures.

A role is missing only if the app has no verified B-or-better move set for the same form and attack type. This compares assessment coverage, not inclusion in the 50-card display limit. PvP and gym recommendations and reachable Mega/evolution roles may already give a missing regular form value. Off-type rows are marked for review; inclusion here is not a recommendation to invest in every entry.
